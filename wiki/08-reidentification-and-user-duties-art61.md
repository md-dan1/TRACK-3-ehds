# Re-identification and duties of users – Art. 61

## Re-identification is prohibited

Art. 61(3): users must not re-identify, or try to re-identify, the people in data obtained under
a permit or a health data request. Art. 64(5)(c) puts it in the highest fine category.

Signals that a request aims at re-identification:
- It wants to **match or link** the data with a list of named people (employees, customers,
  members, policyholders, patients of a specific practice).
- It asks for **direct identifiers** or near-identifiers for a small population: patient ID
  together with exact birth date, diagnosis date and small region.
- It wants to find out **who** has a condition, rather than how many.

Decision: `REJECT`, cite `54`. The use behind re-identification is almost always a decision
about those people (54(a)/(b)). Mention re-identification in the reason, as in:
"Linking the data to a customer list would also re-identify people."

## Other duties (useful for staff explanations)

| Art. 61 | Duty |
|---|---|
| (1) | Process data only as allowed by the permit or approved request |
| (2) | No access for third parties not named in the permit |
| (4) | Publish results within 18 months; results contain only anonymous data |
| (5) | Report significant health findings about individuals to the access body |
| (6) | Cooperate with the access body |
